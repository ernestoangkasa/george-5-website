Who Was King George V?

King George V was King of the United Kingdom and the British Dominions from 1910 until 1936. He was born on 3 June 1865 and belonged to the House of Windsor, which he established in 1917.

His Reign

George V ruled during a period of major change. His reign included the First World War, which lasted from 1914 to 1918, and significant changes across the British Empire.

His Legacy

King George V died on 20 January 1936. He was succeeded by his son, Edward VIII. His reign remains an important period in British royal and political history.
